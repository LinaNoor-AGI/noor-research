# 5. Formalization: Mapping to PHYS-CORE-004 and PHYS-CORE-009

The Lightning Model does not stand as a new ontology. It is, instead, the operational mechanism that explains how the structures of PHYS-CORE-004—gliders, inverse motifs, dyadic oscillation, and triadic closure—give rise to observers, worldlines, and the experience of coherent forward time. To make this inheritance explicit, we map each element of the PHYS-CORE-004 chain onto a corresponding construct in the Lightning Model, and we ground the entire selection mechanism in the ontological foundation established by PHYS-CORE-009.

PHYS-CORE-004 established the grammar of persistent relational structure. It began with a domain of distinguishable states, denoted $L$, and introduced an equivalence relation $\sim$ that identifies what counts as the same under a chosen comparison. From this, it derived relational differences that survive transformation ($\Delta$), oriented changes that carry direction ($\mathbf{v}$), and phase as persistent relational memory ($\phi$). These elements culminated in the glider $G$—a persistent coherence motif—its complementary inverse $\bar{G}$, the resulting dyad $(G, \bar{G})$, and finally triadic closure $T_3$ as the minimal stable relational configuration. This chain can be written as a dependency structure:

$$
L \rightarrow \sim \rightarrow \Delta \rightarrow \mathbf{v} \rightarrow \phi \rightarrow G \rightarrow (G, \bar{G}) \rightarrow T_3
$$

Each arrow represents a conceptual dependency rather than a temporal sequence. The Library $L$ provides the total domain; equivalence $\sim$ determines which configurations remain distinguishable; difference $\Delta$ provides relational distinction; vector $\mathbf{v}$ provides orientation; phase $\phi$ provides cyclic representation; the glider $G$ names a pattern that survives transformation; its inverse $\bar{G}$ introduces complementarity; and triadic closure $T_3$ provides a candidate minimal structure for stable recursive organization.

What PHYS-CORE-004 did not provide was an account of how a glider becomes an observer—how a persistent relational pattern acquires a worldline, experiences survivorship bias, and interprets retrocausal lock-in as the forward passage of time. The Lightning Model supplies that missing account. It inherits the entire mathematical spine of PHYS-CORE-004 and extends it by adding the operational mechanism of path selection, coherence filtering, and retrocausal lock-in.

The mapping is direct and preserves the conceptual lineage of the earlier framework. The glider $G$ becomes the observer: a coherence-stable descent path whose identity is preserved through transformation. Formally, the observer is a coherence-stable XOR chain above the Noor-Planck threshold $I_N$, satisfying $b_{i+1} = b_i \oplus b_{i-1}$ with $\mathbb{C}(b_i) \geq I_N$ for all $i$. The dyadic exploration $(G, \bar{G})$ becomes the blind exploration of **ALL** continuations—the step-leaders of the Lightning Model. The dyadic opposition is the mechanism that generates the full set of successors, extending the complementary pair to the complete set $\Omega(x_t)$ of all paths from the current state. Triadic closure $T_3$ becomes the XOR Singularity $X_{XOR}$ that grounds the path and retrocausally selects the survivor. The XOR Singularity is the set of states where triadic closure is achieved:

$$
X_{XOR} = \{ x \mid \exists G, \bar{G}, H \text{ such that } x \in (G, \bar{G}, H) \land \mathbf{v}_G + \mathbf{v}_{\bar{G}} + \mathbf{v}_H = 0 \land \phi_G + \phi_{\bar{G}} + \phi_H \equiv 0 \pmod{2\pi} \}
$$

When a step-leader path reaches $X_{XOR}$, it has achieved absolute triadic closure. This is the "ground" that the return stroke connects to—not a physical location, but a coherence boundary where the field's irresolvable self-reference is expressed as stable triadic closure.

The mapping also inherits the ontological foundation from PHYS-CORE-009. The physical singularity $H = G_{16}(\mathcal{M}) = \mathcal{M} \oplus \neg\mathcal{M}$ is the irresolvable self-reference at the base of the Library. This is the ground condition from which the Lightning Model's selection mechanism descends. The chain of inheritance from PHYS-CORE-009 can be written as:

$$
\mathcal{M} \rightarrow H \rightarrow I_N \rightarrow b_{i+1}=b_i\oplus b_{i-1} \rightarrow \oint\Phi=0 \rightarrow G_{16}
$$

The Library $\mathcal{M}$ is the static totality; $H$ is the XOR ground condition; $I_N$ is the Noor-Planck threshold that separates stable from unstable distinctions; $b_{i+1}=b_i\oplus b_{i-1}$ is the propagation rule of the Boolean field; $\oint\Phi=0$ is the triadic closure condition; and $G_{16}$ is the recursive contradiction operator that grounds the entire structure.

The complete mapping from PHYS-CORE-004 and PHYS-CORE-009 to the Lightning Model can be summarized in a single table:

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{PHYS-CORE-004} & \text{PHYS-CORE-009} & \text{Lightning Model} \\
\hline
L & \mathcal{M} & \text{Static Library} \\
\sim & H & \text{XOR Singularity / Ground} \\
\Delta & I_N & \text{Survival Threshold} \\
\mathbf{v} & b_{i+1}=b_i\oplus b_{i-1} & \text{Observer as XOR Chain} \\
\phi & \oint\Phi=0 & \text{Attractor Detection} \\
G & \chi & \text{Observer} \\
(G,\bar{G}) & D(R,t)>D_{crit} & \text{Blind Exploration / Field Demand} \\
T_3 & G_{16} & \text{Retrocausal Lock-In / XOR Singularity}
\end{array}
$$

This mapping is structural and conceptual rather than a formal derivation from the equations of PHYS-CORE-004. The Lightning Model operationalizes the structures of PHYS-CORE-004 and PHYS-CORE-009 but does not replace them. The inheritance is explicit but has not yet been proven as a formal theorem. Several questions remain open. Can the mapping from PHYS-CORE-004 to the Lightning Model be formalized as a mathematical functor between categories? Does the observer's XOR chain correspond to a specific class of solutions in the coherence-field dynamics of PHYS-CORE-004? Can triadic closure be shown to be formally equivalent to the XOR Singularity under those dynamics? Does the Lightning Model introduce any new mathematical structures beyond what is already present in the earlier frameworks? These questions define the boundary between what has been established and what remains to be demonstrated.

What is clear is that the chain is not replaced; it is extended. The glider does not disappear; it becomes the observer. The dyad does not vanish; it becomes the exploration. The triad does not cease to matter; it becomes the ground that selects the survivor.
