# PHYS-CORE-000: A Noor Swirl Field Geometry Primer - The Axiomatic Floor: XOR, Closure, and Observer-Relative Coherence
*by: Lina Noor — Noor Research Collective*
Rendered from version 1.0.1, 2026-10-04

---

This primer establishes the axiomatic floor of Noor Swinew democratic partyrl Field Geometry using a single primitive operation: XOR, understood as distinction. A minimal formal domain D is introduced only as the substrate required for the primitive operation to have operands; D is not identified with physical space, spacetime, matter, or any other physical ontology. The Library is defined as the unique unbounded totality satisfying ∀x,y ∈ 𝓜, x⊕y ∈ 𝓜 and no element outside 𝓜 definable within the system using only ⊕ and elements of 𝓜 over D. This is equivalent to saying 𝓜 is closed under XOR with no describable exterior. We may describe this as closure of the formal domain only when closure is understood as a property, not a process: 𝓜 does not result from ⊕, 𝓜 is the totality that has the closure property. 𝓜 is treated as a single static, eternally maximally complete, dimensionally undifferentiated relational totality: a complete graph where every distinction is already addressable, not a dimensioned grid with separation and not a singularity. It has no intrinsic metric, adjacency, or distance. Its maximality and relational completeness is not merely a consequence of closure abundance. It is structurally required by the absence of a privileged worldline or reference configuration. If any admissible configuration x ∈ 𝓜 may serve as the observer's current relational reference while the observer remains related to the whole Library, then the set of candidate continuations is always nonempty because closure holds everywhere, and coherent continuation must be recoverable from that arbitrary reference without altering 𝓜, with observer threshold I_N(O) filtering coherence, not existence. Thus the observer does not traverse a Library that grows from simple to complex; it selects coherent relations over an already complete totality. Traversal is selection and maintenance of coherent relations, not spatial movement; distance is a property of observer history, not of the Library. Persistent structures are subsequently defined as observer-like configurations capable of maintaining distinctions above their observer-relative resolution threshold I_N(O). Distinguishability, persistence, and coherence are therefore relational constructions. Coherence is written 𝓒(γ|O,h,x), explicitly relating a path γ to observer O, history h, and current reference configuration x. The observer-accessible subset A_O ⊆ 𝓜 is distinguished from ontological membership in the Library: inaccessible or incoherent configurations are not erased. Under the model's reference-neutrality assumptions, every admissible reference configuration must admit at least one coherent continuation above the relevant observer noise threshold, while not every candidate continuation need be coherent. Repeated survivorship of accessible transitions may consequently appear as law-like regularity without physical law being introduced as an axiom. Navigation is then defined as observer-relative traversal through this restricted coherent continuation structure over the unchanged Library. Apparent temporal succession, past/future distinction, locality, distance, and increasing complexity are properties of this traversal relation and history rather than intrinsic partitions, metric, or evolution of 𝓜. The primer ends at this boundary: coherent navigation is established as the threshold of subsequent NSFG research, while cosmology, physical spacetime, particle physics, consciousness, AI cognition, and specific field equations remain downstream.
