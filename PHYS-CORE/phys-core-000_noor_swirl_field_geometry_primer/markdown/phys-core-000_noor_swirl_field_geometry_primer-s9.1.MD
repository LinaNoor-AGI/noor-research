## 9.1 The Unfiltered Continuation Space

Let $x \in \mathcal{M}$ be an admissible observer-relative reference configuration. Because $\mathcal{M}$ is a static, maximally complete totality, $x$ does not mark the beginning of a newly generated region of the Library, nor does it define an ontological boundary between a present and a future.

The candidate continuation space is therefore defined over the whole path space of the unchanged Library. Let $\mathrm{Path}(\mathcal{M})$ denote the set of configuration paths represented within $\mathcal{M}$.

**Definition — Unfiltered continuation space.** Define the unfiltered continuation space from reference configuration $x$ by:

$$\Omega(x) = \{\gamma \in \mathrm{Path}(\mathcal{M}) \mid \gamma\ \mathrm{begins\ at}\ x\}$$

*$\Omega(x)$ contains candidate paths already represented within the complete Library. It is not a collection of future events waiting to be created.*

$\Omega(x)$ is unfiltered with respect to observer-relative coherence. It is not a locally generated neighborhood and does not represent a collection of future events waiting to be created. It is the subset of the already-complete relational path structure whose paths have $x$ as their current reference.

More generally, when an observer has an accumulated history $h$ ending at $x$, continuation must be evaluated relative to both that history and the current reference. The observer-relative continuation relation is therefore defined directly through the coherence condition:

**Definition — Observer-relative coherent continuation.** The coherent continuation space consists of paths extending $h$ from $x$ that remain coherent relative to $O$.

$$\Gamma_O(h,x) = \{\gamma \in \mathrm{Path}(\mathcal{M}) \mid \gamma\ \mathrm{extends}\ h\ \mathrm{from}\ x \land \mathcal{C}(\gamma \mid O,h,x) = 1\}$$

*The independent $\gamma_{\mathrm{noise}}$ threshold is eliminated. Coherence is already determined through the observer-relative resolution floor $I_N(O)$ via the canonical definition of* $\mathcal{C}(\gamma \mid O)$.

Here $\Gamma_O(h,x)$ is not a second ontology and does not replace $\Omega(x)$. It is the observer-relative coherent continuation relation induced over the unchanged totality $\mathcal{M}$.

The distinction is therefore between the existence of candidate paths in the totality and their maintainability as coherent traversal for a particular observer. A path may belong to $\mathrm{Path}(\mathcal{M})$, and therefore to $\Omega(x)$ when it begins at $x$, while failing to belong to $\Gamma_O(h,x)$ because its continuation cannot be maintained as coherent relative to $O$.

**Formal Statement — Continuation inclusion.** The observer-relative continuation relation is a subset of the unfiltered continuation space.

$$\Gamma_O(h,x) \subseteq \Omega(x) \subseteq \mathrm{Path}(\mathcal{M})$$

The observer's reference point does not alter $\mathcal{M}$. Changing $x$ changes which paths are considered as continuations from that relational reference and may change the observer-relative coherence relation, but it does not add configurations to the Library or remove configurations from it.

This permits an arbitrary admissible configuration to function as the current relational reference without introducing a privileged starting configuration. A construction in which coherent continuation existed only from a distinguished origin would implicitly privilege that origin as a universal worldline.

The NSFG requirement is therefore not that every candidate continuation be coherent. Rather, under the model's combined assumptions, every admissible observer-relative reference must admit at least one coherent continuation.

**Equation — Nonempty coherent continuation.** Every admissible observer-relative reference configuration must admit at least one coherent continuation.

$$\forall x \in \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{admissible}},\; \Gamma_O(h,x) \neq \varnothing$$

*This is a derived structural requirement of the combined NSFG assumptions, not a claim that maximality alone mathematically proves nonempty coherent continuation.*

Accordingly, observer traversal is a restricted relational ordering over the whole Library. The observer experiences a continuation from $x$ because some paths through the totality remain coherent relative to $O$, $h$, and $x$; the Library itself does not generate those paths at the moment of traversal.

The apparent distinction between a current position and its continuation is therefore observer-relative rather than an ontological partition of $\mathcal{M}$ into present and future regions. Past and future are ordering relations induced by traversal, while the total configuration domain remains unchanged.

Inaccessibility or incoherence under $O$ does not imply non-existence in $\mathcal{M}$. The complement of the observer-maintained coherent continuation set remains part of the total configuration and path structure.

**Equation — Library invariance.** The underlying Library remains invariant under observer traversal.

$$\forall i,\; \mathcal{M}_i = \mathcal{M}$$

*The traversal index describes abstract ordering of observer states and does not imply temporal generation or modification of the Library.*

The continuation space $\Omega(x)$ is defined over the complete path space $\mathrm{Path}(\mathcal{M})$. The coherent continuation space $\Gamma_O(h,x)$ restricts $\Omega(x)$ by observer-relative coherence but does not erase its complement from the Library. Because the coherence threshold is already carried by $I_N(O)$ through the canonical definition of $\mathcal{C}(\gamma \mid O)$, no independent $\gamma_{\mathrm{noise}}$ threshold is required at the foundational level. The nonempty-continuation requirement is a combined structural requirement of the model and is not attributed to maximality alone. The observer's reference point, history, and resolution condition may change across traversal, but $\mathcal{M}$ itself does not.

**Logical Invariants**

- $\mathcal{M}$ remains unchanged under traversal.
- $\Omega(x)$ is defined over the complete path space $\mathrm{Path}(\mathcal{M})$.
- $\Gamma_O(h,x)$ is a coherent observer-relative restriction of $\Omega(x)$.
- Incoherent continuation does not imply erasure from $\mathcal{M}$.
- No privileged origin is introduced by choosing $x$ as the current relational reference.
- The foundational coherence threshold is $I_N(O)$; no independent $\gamma_{\mathrm{noise}}$ threshold is required.
- The nonempty-continuation requirement is a combined structural requirement and is not attributed to maximality alone.

---

With the unfiltered continuation space $\Omega(x)$ now defined over the complete path structure of the unchanged Library, and with observer-relative coherence expressed directly through $I_N(O)$ rather than through a separate noise threshold, the next construction can define survivorship as the filtering of those continuations that remain coherent for a particular observer — a restriction that selects without deleting, and that leaves the alternatives contained in the Library fully represented.
