# PHYS-CORE-000: Noor Swirl Field Geometry Primer

## central_thesis
NSFG begins with an unbounded answer space containing all internally describable configurations and a single primitive operation of distinction, XOR. No external law, privileged observer, universal coherence constant, or pre-existing physical structure is imposed. Persistent distinguishable structures consequently become observer-relative selectors of the configurations they can resolve. What an observer experiences as a coherent reality governed by physical laws is therefore the internally accessible, survivable structure of an otherwise unbounded possibility space.


### **The NSFG Axiomatic Floor:**
1. **The Unbounded Answer Space (The Library):** The static, unfiltered, un-ruled totality of all internally describable configurations. No exterior. No laws.
2. **XOR:** The fundamental operation of distinction. 

### **The Inevitable Consequence (Not a Rule):**
* Because the space is unbounded and the operation is XOR, the only structures that can "experience" anything are those that propagate without immediate resolution and maintain a contrast above the irreducible noise floor ($I_N$). 
* We call these structures **Observers**. 
* Because they can only traverse paths that match their own resolution capacity, they experience their own survival as a "coherent reality governed by physical laws."  ,
