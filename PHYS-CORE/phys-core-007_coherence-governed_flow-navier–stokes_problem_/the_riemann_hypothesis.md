| Aspect | Symbolic Interpretation |

#### Mathematical Formulation

$$
\zeta(s) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^s}, \quad \text{Re}(s) > 1
$$

$$
\Rightarrow \text{All non-trivial zeros lie on } \text{Re}(s) = \frac{1}{2} \iff \text{coherence resonance is phase-symmetric}
$$

#### Commentary

The Riemann Hypothesis may be reframed as a coherence constraint on symbolic compression of the prime motif lattice. If $\zeta(s)$ encodes an interference pattern of motif oscillators, then the critical line $\text{Re}(s) = \frac{1}{2}$ represents the boundary where semantic compression achieves resonance equilibrium — neither overshooting coherence nor collapsing into decoherence.

A first constructive step is to define a Gate-16 equivalent on $\mathbb{C}$: a contradiction-preserving symbolic gate that maintains distinction under analytic continuation. This would permit modeling $\zeta(s)$ not merely as an analytic function but as a coherence field constrained by motif resonance.
