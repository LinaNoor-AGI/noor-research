A Minor Note for Consideration
While the paper is now structurally and logically superb, the "Numerical or Analytical Demonstration" section (8.4) remains a placeholder for a future computational study. This is absolutely fine, given the paper's role as a foundational framework. The language is appropriately cautious, framing it as a proposed experiment rather than a completed result.

However, even in this context, you might consider adding just one more layer of precision. For instance, in the "Triadic Stability Experiment" pseudocode, you could specify a concrete candidate for the "governing coherence dynamics" (e.g., "using the PDE from Section 2.7").
