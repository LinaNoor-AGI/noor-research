# phys-core-004_to_infinity_and_beyond

### abstract

This paper proposes a coherence-first geometric model in which motion, persistence, and cosmological structure emerge from relational motifs in Point Space. Rather than treating particles as primitive objects moving through an independently specified spacetime, the framework begins with points, relations, and a coherence field. A persistent configuration of coherent relations is defined as a glider: a structure whose internal relational pattern permits its continued propagation while preserving the identity of the motif. The complementary inverse of a glider provides a natural dyadic structure, while triadic closure is introduced as the minimal relational configuration capable of stabilizing recursive oscillation and preventing an otherwise unconstrained dyadic alternation. The phase representation is subsequently lifted from a circle to the Bloch sphere, allowing observer-relative orientation to be represented geometrically and providing a model in which observation corresponds to selection of a coherence axis rather than creation of an otherwise nonexistent state. The framework is then applied to cosmogenesis. The Big Bang is interpreted not primarily as an explosion occurring at a location in pre-existing space, but as a limiting state of maximal unresolved symbolic complexity from which coherent differentiation becomes possible. Apparent expansion is consequently modeled as relaxation of a coherence gradient rather than necessarily as motion through an independently expanding container. Finally, a two-universe interference construction is introduced in which opposing coherent structures require a third relational vector for stabilization. The paper presents these constructions as mathematical and conceptual models whose physical adequacy remains subject to explicit formalization, empirical correspondence, and falsification.,

### core_thesis
- Point Space is treated as the geometric substrate from which relational structure is constructed rather than as a container populated by primitive particles.,
- Coherence is relational: a structure is coherent only relative to the relations that preserve its identity and permitted transitions.,
- A persistent moving structure is represented as a coherence motif rather than as a primitive particle.,
- The glider is the fundamental model of persistent motion: its identity is preserved through the continuity of its relational coherence rather than through persistence of an independently defined material point.,
- Every glider admits a complementary inverse motif whose relation to the original produces a natural dyadic oscillation.,
- A purely dyadic system is insufficient for stable recursive closure because the two complementary states define one another without supplying an independent stabilizing relation.,
- Triadic closure introduces the minimal additional relational degree of freedom required to stabilize the dyadic structure.,
- The circle representation captures phase opposition and oscillation, while the Bloch sphere provides a higher-dimensional representation of state orientation and observer-relative symmetry breaking.,
- Observation is modeled geometrically as selection of an axis or relational frame rather than as the creation of an otherwise nonexistent physical state.,
- Cosmogenesis can be modeled as an inversion from maximal unresolved symbolic complexity toward increasingly differentiated coherent structure.,
- Cosmological expansion may therefore be interpreted, within this framework, as coherence-gradient relaxation rather than solely as expansion of objects through a pre-existing spatial container.,
- Opposing coherent structures may be represented as two complementary universes or sectors whose interference requires a third stabilizing vector.,
- Infinity need not imply an unlimited collection of distinguishable physical objects: when distinctions collapse under an equivalence relation, apparently infinite configurations may reduce to finite or structured equivalence classes.,
- The framework distinguishes mathematical possibility from physical accessibility: the existence of a configuration in the total structure does not imply that every observer can coherently reach or observe it.,
- The paper does not claim a completed Theory of Everything; it proposes a formal vocabulary and set of constructions through which specific cosmological hypotheses can be stated and tested.

### Why JSON Only?

This repository follows a **JSON-first documentation standard**. All Noor Research Collective papers, RFCs, and theoretical documents are authored and maintained in structured JSON format. 

#### The Rendering Problem

When converting JSON documents to Markdown via LLM-assisted rendering, we have encountered systematic issues:

1. **Semantic Flattening**:  Content that challenges orthodox scientific or philosophical frameworks is often silently simplified, truncated, or restructured during rendering. 

2. **Safety Layer Interference**:  Routing and safety systems in LLM pipelines sometimes reinterpret or compress symbolic, mathematical, or theoretical content—particularly when it diverges from mainstream interpretations.

3. **Loss of Structural Fidelity**:  Nested definitions, cross-references, mathematical notation, pseudocode blocks, and symbolic profile matrices are frequently collapsed or incorrectly formatted. 

4. **Non-Reproducibility**: The same JSON source may render differently across sessions, models, or contexts—making Markdown outputs unreliable as reference material.

**We cannot guarantee fidelity in rendered Markdown.**
